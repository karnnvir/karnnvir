### Focus on contacts Avoidance than on Agent Efficiency   
    
This year I inverted the customer service funnel for international markets. We'd been spending most of our effort at the bottom — making agents faster at handling contacts. I moved the weight to the top: avoid the contact entirely, then automate self-serve, then optimise the agent experience last.

**The diagnosis was unit economics, and experience.** A contact that reaches an agent costs about three dollars. Self-serving it through a bot costs about fifty cents. Avoiding it altogether costs nothing to serve. And the agent contact is the only one where the customer has already had a bad experience — that's where CSAT and NPS take the hit. So we were spending the most exactly where the return was smallest, and almost nothing at the top.

**Two things gave me the evidence.** Last year I built a GenAI-based customer insights capability — anomaly detection and issue trending across customer feedback and conversations, feeding root causes to the teams who owned the fix. It proved itself during the Canada migration from our legacy stack onto the Walmart commerce platform, and returned $15M in retained GMV. That's what convinced me the top of the funnel was where the leverage was. On the resolution side we had signal too: a purely deterministic voicebot in Canada — no GenAI at all — reached 10% resolution, and RAG-based answers to informational questions took Mexico chat resolution from 30% to 35%.

**Three pillars, in priority order.**

**Avoid** — a global insights platform taking in customer feedback and conversations, surfacing anomaly- and trend-based issues, and handing the owning teams exactly what to fix proactively.

**Automate** — GenAI self-serve across voicebot and chatbot. Not deterministic — probabilistic, conversational, powered by our knowledge bases plus live order information and action APIs, so the bot can actually resolve rather than escalate.

**Optimise** — agent tooling last. An AI assist that gives the agent the proposed answer and the next steps for multi-step resolutions like a cancellation or a return. The agent takes it as-is, modifies it, or writes their own — plus a handover summary of the customer's bot conversation so nobody repeats themselves.

With this strategy, we invested and focused more on the top 2 priorities - Avoid and Automate to completely avoid the contacts altogether and allow customers to self serve for their queries
